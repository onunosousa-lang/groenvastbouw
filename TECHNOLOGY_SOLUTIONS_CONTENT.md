# Senmar Solutions Page Content Structure

## Page Overview
The Senmar solutions page presents 4 technical solution tiers for prefabricated construction:

### Two Construction Methods Explained:
1. **Panelized Construction**: Pre-manufactured walls and panels, fabricated off-site, assembled on-site like building blocks
2. **Modular Construction**: Entire sections/modules created off-site with all systems, assembled on-site like puzzle pieces

---

## Solution Tier 1: Basic Structure
**Starting from 190€/m²**

Description: Foundational solution for building frame. Great for those looking to reduce costs and have expertise to complete construction themselves.

### Components:
- **External OSB plate**: OSB-3, Load-bearing, moisture-resistant, 12mm
- **Timber frame structure**: Kiln dried (16%), Treated, Calibrated (C24), 195x45mm
- **Joist beam**: Kiln dried (16%), Planned, Calibrated, Graduated (C24), 390x45mm
- **Purlins**: Kiln dried (16%), Planned, Calibrated, Graduated (C24), 195x195mm
- **Closing board**: Kiln dried (16%), Planned, Calibrated, Graduated (C24), 195x45mm
- **Rafters**: Kiln dried (16%), Treated, Calibrated, Graduated (C24), 195x45mm

---

## Solution Tier 2: Optimal Structure
**Starting from 270€/m²**

Description: Designed for moderate climates. Building frame includes 200mm of insulation and internal vapor barrier. Guarantees comfortable microclimate and supports structural integrity. Perfect for comfortable year-round living in regions with mild winter conditions.

### Key Features:
- 200mm Primary insulation (Soft mineral wool)
- U-value = 0.035 W/mK
- R-value = 5.7 W/m²K
- Fire safety class A
- Internal vapor barrier membrane

### Components Include:
- External OSB plate (12mm)
- Timber frame structure
- Primary insulation (200mm soft mineral wool)
- Vapor barrier membrane (diffusion, 95gr/m²)
- OSB plate (9mm)
- OSB flooring board (22mm)
- Shock absorbing tape (3.2mm)
- Joist beam
- Battens and counter battens
- Wind-moisture membrane (120gr/m²)
- Rafters and purlins

## Solution Tier 3: Passive Structure
**Starting from 320€/m²**

Description: Features additional external insulation for building frame, with thickness adjusted according to local climate conditions. Enhanced thermal protection results in cooler home during summer and reduced heating energy in winter. Over next decade, substantial savings on heating and cooling costs. Well-suited for regions with harsh winters and very hot climates.

### Key Features:
- External insulation (Hard mineral wool)
- U-value = 0.035 W/mK
- R-value = 2.8 W/m²K (external)
- 200mm Primary insulation (internal)
- R-value = 5.7 W/m²K (internal)
- 400mm insulation in roof
- R-value = 11.4 W/m²K (roof)

### Components Include:
- Vertical timber battens (120x45mm)
- External insulation (Hard mineral wool)
- Wind-moisture membrane
- External OSB plate
- Timber frame structure
- Primary insulation (200mm soft mineral wool)
- Vapor barrier membrane
- OSB flooring board
- Shock absorbing tape
- Joist beam
- Battens and counter battens
- Roof insulation (400mm soft mineral wool)
- Rafters with 390x45mm dimensions
